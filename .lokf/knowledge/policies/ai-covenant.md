# AI covenant

A person owns everything they contribute, whatever tools helped.[^ai_covenant.md] An element of the schema says what FINOS's files say, and a rule states only what FINOS states.[^ai_covenant.md]

AI systems must not post directly in issues or discussions.[^ai_covenant.md]
